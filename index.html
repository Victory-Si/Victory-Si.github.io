01 · About

Research that moves across environments.

Beginning in July 2026, I will join the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University as a postdoctoral researcher under the Shuimu Scholar Program, working with Prof. Yuan Shen on localization, sensing, and networked intelligence.

I pursued my integrated Master–Doctor training in Information and Communication Engineering at UESTC, ranking in the top 5% of my cohort, under the supervision of Prof. Xiansheng Guo. During my undergraduate study at North University of China, I was advised by Prof. Xingling Shao. I have also collaborated with Prof. Nirwan Ansari and Prof. Dusit Niyato. From November 2024 to October 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. researcher at Queen Mary University of London under Prof. Yuanwei Liu and Dr. Jin Zhang.

02 · Research

Three connected directions

From physical signals to cooperative intelligence.

01

Data-driven positioning

Crowdsourcing and trajectory-driven learning, reinforcement learning for positioning, and knowledge transfer for long-term adaptive indoor localization.

  • Crowdsourcing
  • Trajectory learning
  • Domain adaptation
02

Array sensing

Continuous-aperture array modeling, direction-of-arrival estimation, MUSIC algorithms, and Cramér–Rao lower-bound analysis for next-generation wireless sensing.

  • CAPA
  • DOA estimation
  • MUSIC & CRLB
03

Intelligent transportation

Automated valet parking and charging, parking-lot digital twins, multi-vehicle cooperative localization, and reinforcement-learning-based trajectory planning.

  • AVP & charging
  • Digital twins
  • Multi-vehicle systems
