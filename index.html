Postdoctoral Researcher · Shuimu Scholar · Zhang Keqian Named Postdoctoral Fellow

I work on wireless localization and intelligent sensing, with a focus on cross-scenario generalization, crowdsourced and cooperative positioning, and array signal processing. I received my Ph.D. from UESTC in 2026 and was a visiting researcher at Queen Mary University of London.

Research interests: Indoor localization · Foundation models · Cooperative sensing · DOA estimation
